## Recipe for cooking omena
Ingredients:
- 2 cups of omena (small dried fish)
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 cloves of garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon of salt
Preparation:
1. Rinse the omena thoroughly in cold water to remove any dirt or impurities.
